The Discovery Crisis

Why Legacy Recommendation Engines Fail

The three systemic bottlenecks in modern entertainment platforms

FLAW 01
The Static Matrix Trap
Traditional Collaborative Filtering (SVD/ALS) flattens user history into a static matrix. It cannot capture chronological momentum (e.g. watching an action sequel 10 minutes after part 1 vs. 6 months ago).
FLAW 02
The Cold-Start Blindspot
When a new film releases (e.g. 2026 title with 0 ratings) or a new user joins, matrix decomposition produces zero vectors, causing recommendations to completely break or serve spam.
FLAW 03
Fragile Neural Services
Deep learning microservices are computationally heavy. When a GPU runs out of VRAM or encounters a timeout spike, traditional systems crash, resulting in empty rows and high churn.
💡 The Opportunity: A unified hierarchical engine that unites sequential attention with an instant circuit breaker.
